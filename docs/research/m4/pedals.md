# Expression pedals [verified offline]

The two expression pedal jacks, their positions and events, the class-6 listener that drives EXP 1 and EXP 2, TogglEXP, and EXP Position's re-drive at a preset load. Status tags as in [conventions.md](../conventions.md). Controllers and their assignments: [footswitches.md](footswitches.md). The feature that shows the pedals on Home: [features/exp-display.md](../features/exp-display.md).

- **The jacks** [verified offline, by disassembly]: a record per jack at `0x281f5d98 + 0x14 * jack` (0 tip, 1 ring): +0 and +4 the raw minimum and maximum the calibration tracks, +8 the position last posted (float 0..1, after calibration and polarity: what the controller got), +0x11 the polarity, +0x12 a pedal present (`0x281f5daa` tip, `0x281f5dbe` ring; set after about 0.3 s of readings under 1000, cleared by one at or above). The mode bytes `0x281f5d89` (tip) and `0x281f5d88` (ring), 0 for EXP, else FS, are Global Settings > Preferences' Tip and Ring EXP/FS (`0x5f`, `0x60`, written at `0x28db921c`). An absent or not yet swept pedal reads 0, -0.0 (bits `0x80000000`) uncalibrated, or 1.0 inverted: issue #99's 100 %.
- **Events** [verified offline]: the ADC task (`0x28db8c00`, every 10 ms) posts `{6, jack, 0, x}` when x moved by at least 0.01 (`0x28db9908`) or reached exactly 0 or 1, then stores +8; nothing while the pedal is still. The key queue keeps one class-6 event per jack: a newer one replaces a queued one's value ([footswitches.md](footswitches.md)). Plug-in (x stays 0), an unplug at the heel, a TogglEXP change, a jack's EXP/FS change and a preset's re-drive under EXP Position Global (below) post no event.
- **The listener** [verified offline]: the literal `0x28d96d7c` registers `0x28d947d1` for class 6 (`0x28d96b58..0x28d96b64`); `0x28d947d0(ev, ctx)` sends ev[1] 0 to controller 1 and 1 to controller 2 with the float at ev + 4 (a tail call of `0x28dde408(0x28125d30, ctl, 0, x)`), and returns for any other ev[1]. While Controller or Bypass Assign learns (`[0x281281e8]`, byte `0x4b8` of `0x28127d30`) it tail-calls the learn `0x28ddf6dc` instead, which for controllers 1 and 2 clears that byte (`0x28ddf6fa..0x28ddf6fc`: `ctl - 3` is above 4) and gives the controller nothing. So a handler after the listener always reads the byte as 0, for the event that ended a learn too; a handler before it reads it as the listener does. The listener runs on the key-queue drain task under the listeners' lock (`0x28db8830`).
- **TogglEXP** [verified offline]: the state `0x28128a6c` (getter `0x28d96898`, setter `0x28d968a4`) is 0 (no TogglEXP footswitch), 1 (EXP 1 active) or 2 (EXP 2 active). The ADC task sends the tip's event to controller 2 while the state is 2 and the ring drives nothing (ring in FS mode or no ring pedal, `0x28db9084..0x28db90a6`, for the tip only); the ring drives controller 2 whenever it is in EXP mode with a pedal. A toggle (the footswitch case at `0x28dd17fc..0x28dd1838`) re-drives nothing, so the newly active controller keeps its value until the pedal moves 1 %.
- **EXP Position** [verified offline, value table `0x28e27c94`]: EXP 1 Position is setting `0x42` and EXP 2 Position `0x47` (EXP Pedals' page table `0x28e3b548`, `{0x42, 0x47, 0x17e}`; the restore below reads `0x42` for controller 1 at `0x28de11d0` and `0x47` for controller 2 at `0x28de11ae`), each Snapsht (0), Preset (1) or Global (2). Under Global, the controller restore `0x28de0f24` sets `0x281281ea` or `0x281281eb` (`0x28de11ac..0x28de11e2`), then drives controller 1 (the `bl` at `0x28de12ba`) and controller 2 (the `bl` at `0x28de1282`) through `0x28dde408(0x28125d30, ctl, 0, x)` from a fresh read `0x28db9270(jack, &x)` (the tip for 1, the ring for 2), with no event, without the TogglEXP remap and without storing +8 (`0x28de124e..0x28de12be`). Snapshot select `0x28de2f90` reads both Positions (`0x28de301c..0x28de3034`) and, going through the recalled snapshot's controller entries, recalls the value of a parameter on EXP 1 only while EXP 1 Position is Snapsht (`0x28de33f0`) and of one on EXP 2 only while EXP 2 Position is (`0x28de3348`); under Preset and Global a snapshot recall leaves them as they are. So under Snapsht or Preset a preset load, and under Snapsht a snapshot recall, give the parameters their stored values, not the pedal's, until the pedal moves. `0x28de0f24` has two callers, `0x28deb2aa` in `0x28deb1f4` (called by `0x28df1fa4`) and `0x28deb8fe` in `0x28deb4e8` (called twice by `0x28df2234`), each of which parses a preset's sections, the controllers, then the footswitches (`0x28de6f48`) [hypothesis: these are the preset loads; [features/exp-display.md](../features/exp-display.md) hooks both].
- **No value per controller** [verified offline]: `0x28dde408` walks the controller's assignments and sets each parameter to Min + (Max - Min) · x; it keeps no word for the controller's own position, so the last call's x is known only to a hook on its callers. The calls for controllers 1 and 2 (a scan of the code segment for a `bl` to `0x28dde408`) are the listener's tail call and the two re-drive calls; the other two drive controller 8 (MIDI CC bypass, `0x28d9b91a`) and the footswitch controllers (`0x28dd123a`).
- **MIDI CC 1 and 2** [verified offline]: the MIDI receive's `tbh` at `0x28d9b134` indexes by CC - 1; cases 0 and 1 go to `0x28db956c(ch, v)`, which posts `{6, ch, 0, v / 127}` (`0x28d9b4e6`, `0x28d9b4d6`): the jack's event, so MIDI CC 1 and 2 move EXP 1 and EXP 2 through the listener. They do not store +8.
- **From the host** [verified offline]: [USB input](../features/usb-input.md) puts any class on the key queue, so `hx_input.event(6, jack, float bits)` moves EXP 1 (jack 0) or EXP 2 (jack 1) as the listener's event, without the TogglEXP remap.
