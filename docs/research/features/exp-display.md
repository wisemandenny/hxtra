# EXP display (`HX_EXP_DISPLAY`, `tools/features/exp_display.py`, `src/m4/exp_display.s`)

EXP 1 and EXP 2 on the Home screen: a vertical bar at each edge of the screen and, above the divider beside the signal flow, "EXP1" or "EXP2" over the value 0..100. Status tags as in [conventions.md](../conventions.md). The stock pedal path it reads (the jack records, the events, the listener and its learn, TogglEXP's remap, EXP Position's re-drive, MIDI CC 1 and 2): [m4/pedals.md](../m4/pedals.md); the Home screen's views and painting: [m4/home-screen.md](../m4/home-screen.md); the fonts and the text renderer: [m4/text-and-fonts.md](../m4/text-and-fonts.md); the GR meter it moves aside for: [gr-meter.md](gr-meter.md).

- **Decisions** (2026-11-18): a bar and a number, always on, on the Home screen only. Left and right of the screen, 6 px wide, from the bottom edge (0) to the row under the divider (100), which stays stock. The label in a 3x5 pixel font at 2x (no stock ASCII font is under 21 px), the value without a `%`, both in the pedal's colour: EXP 1 red `0xff0000`, EXP 2 green `0x43ff40`, the colours stock TogglEXP lights. Values per controller (EXP 1 and EXP 2 as the parameters see them), not per jack. Moves aside for the GR meter (the GR feature exports where it is, below). Long footswitch names may overlap a bar's inner 3 px. The step stays stock (1 %). Global Settings > Displays: Home EXP Left and Home EXP Right, each Off, EXP 1 or EXP 2 (defaults EXP 1 and EXP 2), virtual IDs `0xe9` and `0xea`, bits 0..7 and 8..15 of container 116, the last container (`tools/setting_ids.py`); eight bits each, so a later source such as a MIDI CC number fits without moving the IDs. Opt-in on the setup page (`WEB` `'opt-in'`). Any MIDI CC beyond 1 and 2 as a source is left for later.
- **Decisions after exp-dev2** (2026-11-19, from its run on the unit, below): the value in `hx28_boldFont` (21 px of pixels, up from 16), but in `hx21_regularFont` while the GR meter shows at Middle at L or XL, and in either font at least 2 rows above the GR band's top row; the label unchanged. Under EXP Position Preset or Snapsht, after a preset load (and under Snapsht a snapshot recall) the bar shows only its outline, in the pedal's colour, while the value keeps showing where the pedal is, until the pedal (or MIDI) moves the controller again.

## What it draws [verified offline]

Rows count up from the screen's bottom edge (row 0), x from the left (0..319), as the framebuffer and the ROM fill take them.

- **Bars**: left x 0..5, right x 314..319, rows 0..116 (117 px). A dark track `0x2b2b2b` with 1 px ticks `0x505050` at 25, 50 and 75, the fill in the source's colour over them. A value v fills `(v * h + 50) / 100` rows from the bottom; tick k sits on the row a fill of 25k would start on next, so a fill of exactly 25 leaves its tick showing and anything above covers it. An absent source draws an empty 1 px outline in grey `0x666666`, a stale one the same outline in the source's colour. Each paint fills every pixel of a bar once: the fill, then the track between the ticks and the ticks above it, or the outline's four sides around a black inside; so a bar drawn again as it was changes no pixel, and one that changes changes each pixel once.
- **Readouts**: a 44 px wide box (`BOX_W`, the big font's "100") from the value's lowest row to row 155: rows 124..155 for the big font, 129..155 for the small one; left from x 1 (x 1..44), right up to x 318 (x 275..318). The box is wiped black before a draw only when what it shows (value, source, or state, stale reading as live) or its place or font changed, and before each top view paint; otherwise the label and the value are drawn again over themselves, which changes no pixel, where a wipe first could show for a frame with the breathing line repainting Home 40 times a second [hypothesis: the screen may show a buffer while it is painted, bpm.md's half-drawn dot]. The label's 3x5 glyphs (E, X, P, 1 and 2; `-` is in `exp_glyphs` for drawing the value in the pixel font, unused while it fits in a stock font) at 2x, rows 146..155, 2 px apart (30 px for "EXP1"); the value with the text renderer, anchor 0, mode 1 (only the glyphs' own pixels are written), NUL-terminated, its pixels' highest row at 144, one row under the label: rows 124..144 in `hx28_boldFont` (font 2), rows 129..144 in `hx21_regularFont` (font 1). The left one starts at its box's left edge, the right one ends at its box's right edge. Frozen and absent sources are grey; absent shows "--"; a stale one keeps its colour and its value.
- **Clear of**: the divider (rows 117..118), the signal flow's input and output markers (x 68..76, 244..252; the boxes stay within x 0..66 and 253..319, which the build checks for the meter at Left or Right XL), the preset name (rows 156..196), and the GR band at Middle (below). Footswitch labels reach x 3 and 317 (centred at 53 and 267, up to ±50): a long one overlaps a bar's inner 3 px, accepted.
- **With the GR meter** (Mtr Size T px while it shows): Top changes nothing. Bottom: the bars start at row T + 1. Left: the left bar at x T + 1..T + 6, the left readout from x T + 2. Right: the right bar at x 313 - T..318 - T, the right readout up to x 317 - T. Middle: 100 at row 118 - T/2 - 2, with one row between it and the band, and the value in the small font from T 15. The bar keeps 0..100 over its height. So one column is left between a bar and the meter and two between a readout and it.
- **The GR band at Middle** [verified offline: `gr_meter.s` `layout`, and the ROM fill by disassembly]: each light is a rectangle widget at y = (T + 1) // 2 + 118, T px high, with layer 6, and the widget draw (`0x100019f0`) fills `0x100017e8(fb, x, y, w, h, colour, 6)`, which anchor 6 places on rows [y - h, y) (`0x1000193a`: the row offset is h). So the band covers rows 116..120 at S (5 px), 113..122 at M, 111..125 at L and 108..127 at XL (`gr.middle_rows`), and nothing above: no border, and the divider's four segments it takes are those same widgets. The value's lowest row clears the top row by 4 at S (124 - 120), 2 at M (124 - 122), 4 at L (129 - 125) and 2 at XL (129 - 127). exp-dev2's value took rows 128..143 in every state: 1 row above XL's top row, with no row between, and 3 above L's.
- **The value**: `round(x * 100)`, halves up, from the float's bits in integers (`percent`), the sign bit ignored, 100 at and above 1.0.

## The value fonts [verified offline, from the stock Entries 7 and 8]

`exp_display.metrics()` measures both at build time. `hx28_boldFont` (font 2): digits 0..9 15, 8, 13, 13, 14, 13, 14, 13, 14, 14 px wide, `-` 13; spacing 3 (its record's word 5); every digit's pixels in rows 5..25 of the 28 px cell (`-` in rows 14..16); "100" is 44 px, "--" 29. `hx21_regularFont` (font 1): digits 10 px wide but `1`, 5 px; `-` 10; spacing 3; pixels in rows 4..19 of the 21 px cell (`-` in rows 11..12); "100" 31 px, "--" 23. `exp_display.fonts()` puts each cell so that its highest row of pixels is 144 (cell rows 119 and 125), and the generated `exp.inc` carries, per font, its widths, spacing, font number, cell row and lowest row (`FONTS`), with `BOX_W` and `SMALL_FROM`. `SMALL_FROM` is the smallest Mtr Size whose band the big font's lowest row would clear by under `CLEARANCE` (2) rows: 15 with the stock fonts, so L and XL take the small font. The build stops if the small font does not clear a band it is used under, or a box would leave the sides of the signal flow (from row 120 up, x up to 66 and from 253, with the meter at Left or Right XL).

## Hooks and data [verified offline]

- **`exp_event`**, before the listener (`chain_ptr(0x28d96d7c, 0x28d947d1)`): for ev[1] 0 or 1 and no learn, the event's float with bit 31 set (`VAL`: a value came; -0.0 reads as 0) and the tick's ms count (`STAMP`), then `DIRTY`. Plain word stores: no lock, no call. Before, not after: the learn the listener ends in clears the Learn byte for controllers 1 and 2 ([m4/pedals.md](../m4/pedals.md)), so a handler after it would cache the event that ended a learn, which no parameter got.
- **`exp_redrive`**, after the two calls of the controller setter in a preset's re-drive under EXP Position Global (`chain_bl` at `0x28de12ba` for EXP 1 and `0x28de1282` for EXP 2, to `0x28dde408`): the value the controller got, as `exp_event` stores it, stamped 3 s old (`MS - 3000`), so the re-drive changes the value without making a side live: with no pedal the side stays absent. So under Position Global the display follows the parameters across preset changes, TogglEXP's swap included (the re-drive has no remap). No other call drives EXP 1 or EXP 2 without an event. Both handlers clear their controller's `CHANGED` word.
- **`exp_loaded`**, before the controller restore `0x28de0f24` at its two calls (`chain_bl` at `0x28deb2aa` and `0x28deb8fe`) [hypothesis: the preset loads, [m4/pedals.md](../m4/pedals.md)], and **`exp_recalled`**, after snapshot select (`chain_entry(0x28de2f90)`, its first four bytes `push.w {r4-r11,lr}`): a byte each in both controllers' `CHANGED` words (byte 0 a load, byte 1 a recall), then `DIRTY`. Plain byte and word stores: no lock, no call. Before the restore, as its re-drive under Global, which runs inside it, clears the mark again. More snapshots owns the calls of select a footswitch and MIDI CC 69 make (`own_call`) and calls select itself; the entry hook takes every select, theirs included, so the two coexist (a build with both, and one with every feature: `tests/test_exp_display.py`). A select that recalls nothing marks too: a busy one, an index above 2, and the first visit of a snapshot never saved, which select fills with a copy of the current one instead (its record's valid byte +0x24b is 0, [m4/snapshots.md](../m4/snapshots.md): `0x28de3018` → `0x28de3294`, which ends after the copy), such as More snapshots' bank B opened for the first time; under Snapsht the bar then shows its outline though no parameter changed.
- **`exp_tick`**, after the LED task's 1 ms sleep (`chain_bl(0x28db7f44, 0x28e24c58)`, beside BPM's and the breathing line's): counts ms; compares the mode and present bytes, and TogglEXP with each controller's "event in the last 3 s", with their copies, and sets `DIRTY` on a change, as none of those posts an event; on a TogglEXP change it notes the ms (`TOGGLED`). While `DIRTY` and a side is on, it asks for a Home repaint `0x28db8509(0, 1)` at most every 25 ms, with no "Home painted lately" condition, as the GR meter asks: with such a condition the display would stay still for a user without the breathing line or the BPM dot, which keep Home repainting. No request before the first paint has read the settings. It asks in any view, as the GR meter does (the paint draws only on Home).
- **Paints**: before and after both the Home view's paint (literal `0x28d96e28` → `0x28dc8d39`) and the top view's (literal `0x28d96d90` → `0x28dca359`), as the readouts lie in the top view's area. `ORDER` 115: after the GR meter (90), whose words it reads at build time, and after Preset LED Color (110), so its settings follow that feature's in Displays (page 2 and 3 of a build with Tap LED Color and Preset LED Color).
  - The before-handler reads the two settings, tests whether it may draw (list A's head `0x281f35b8` paints the Home view `0x2812a05c` and list B's head `0x281f3d2c` the top view `0x2812aae0`, the first active node's view, as `gr_meter.s` `showing` reads them; the readouts only while the top view's byte +9, its bank view, is 0 as well) and works out where each side goes. The bank view's three rows of text take rows 126..233 from x 7 [verified offline, by disassembly: the top view's constructor `0x28dca70a..0x28dca7b2`, tops 234, 194 and 154 from the table `0x28e3abd0`, anchor 6, fonts 2 and 3]: over the readouts' boxes, clear of the bars' rows 0..116, so under it the bars go on following the pedals and the GR meter. A Home paint wipes black whatever it drew where nothing will now go (a side turned Off, the GR meter moved), before the stock paint and the meter's draw, though not a readout under the bank view; a top view paint wipes the readouts before each of its paints, which that paint then has to itself, so the bank view's text never lands on a readout, and leaves the bars alone. While it may not draw, a Home paint wipes nothing, so a view in list B over Home keeps its pixels.
  - **Labels after a move**: when a Home paint moves a bar sideways (the GR meter at Left or Right appearing, going, or changing size), its wipe cuts a 6 px black gap through any footswitch label under the old place, and the stock redraws only dirty widgets, so the before-handler then runs the stock Home layout `0x28dc87cc(home, 0)`, as the GR meter does 64 frames after it first shows [verified on unit for the GR meter]: the labels are laid out again and the stock paint that follows redraws them. Not while the meter is at Middle: the layout puts the divider's segments back as stock, and the meter at Middle lights those segments. There the before-handler marks dirty instead, under the widget lock, the node (byte +0x24) of each shown widget of the labels, as `breathing_line.s` marks the divider's segments: the nine text widgets at home + 0x64, + 0xf4 and + 0x1ec, 0x30 apart (two lines and a preset number per footswitch; visible byte +0x2a, node +0x2c), and the 18 image widgets from home + 0x2b0, 0x24 apart (visible +0x1d, node +0x20), which the Home constructor `0x28dc91d0` makes and the layout sets (`0x28dc853c`) [verified offline, by disassembly]. The stock paint that follows erases and draws each of them again. In the usual order the meter's before-handler (ORDER 90) has erased its Left or Right lights earlier in the same paint, so this also mends the cut that erase leaves in the labels. When the display reads a newer `LAYOUT` than the one the meter laid out by (the frame thread's race under The GR meter's words, below), the meter erases its lights a paint later, in which no bar moves, so that cut stays [hypothesis: reasoned from the handlers' order, not seen on the unit]. A hidden one is left alone, as its erase would black out what lies over it ([more-snapshots.md](more-snapshots.md), renamed snapshots' labels). Not yet seen on the unit. A bar that only moves up or down keeps its columns, so it needs neither; a side turned Off leaves no black in a long label: the setting is changed in Global Settings, and the return to Home draws the labels whole [verified on unit, 2026-11-20: Stomp mode, FS1's label from x 2, Home EXP Left set to Off by hand, the label's columns 0..5 lit at once and still after 2 s].
  - The after-handler draws each side that is on: the bar, then the readout (the label's fills, the value under the widget lock as `more_snapshots.s` draws its text, after a wipe of its box when that is due, above). A Home paint draws the bars where they go now; a top view paint draws them where they are, so only a Home paint moves a bar, after its wipe and layout. ROM fill `0x100017e8` (anchor 0), text renderer `0x28db7aa4` (owned by the boot screen, which hands other text to the stock one).
- **States**, per controller at paint time, in this order: absent, frozen, stale, live. Live while a jack with a pedal drives it now (tip: EXP mode, a pedal, and the remap's controller; ring: EXP mode and a pedal, for EXP 2). Else, while TogglEXP is 1 or 2 with a pedal on the tip in EXP mode, it is not the tip's controller and it has a value: live while its last event is under 3 s old and came after the last TogglEXP change (MIDI CC moving the controller the tip left), else frozen (grey, last value); so a toggle greys the side it left at once, and a grey side never moves. With no pedal on the tip, no pedal left either controller. Else live while its last event is under 3 s old; else absent. A live controller is stale while its `CHANGED` word holds a load mark and its Position (setting `0x42` for EXP 1, `0x47` for EXP 2, read at each paint) is Snapsht or Preset, or a recall mark and its Position is Snapsht: select recalls EXP-assigned parameters only under Snapsht (`0x28de33f0`, `0x28de3348`, [m4/pedals.md](../m4/pedals.md)). Absent and frozen come first: an absent side has no value to show, and a frozen one already says the pedal is not moving it. A driven controller with no value yet (no event, no re-drive) takes its jack's +8 at that paint (`SEED`, once), so the first paint, not the boot, seeds it: before the ADC task's first pass +8 reads 0, and boot drops the first events.
- **Limits** [verified offline, by disassembly: [m4/pedals.md](../m4/pedals.md)]: no word holds a controller's value, so the display knows only the events and the re-drives it hooks. Under EXP Position Snapsht or Preset, a preset change (and under Snapsht a snapshot change) gives the parameters their stored values without either: the value keeps showing the last one it saw (the pedal's, or MIDI's), and the bar shows only its outline until the next event. A mark taken under Global with no re-drive (no assignment to that controller) stays until the next event, so turning that Position to Preset or Snapsht then shows the outline. The other way, Global hides a mark without clearing it: a Position turned to Global after a load under Preset or Snapsht fills the bar again while the parameters keep the stored values, as nothing re-drives them. A preset with no controller section skips the restore (`0x28deb6dc`, `0x28deb232`) and leaves no mark [hypothesis: `0x28de05c8`, called instead, clears the assignments, so nothing follows EXP 1 or EXP 2 there]. A seed is the pedal's position: after a TogglEXP change under those Positions, a controller seeded then shows where the pedal is, while its parameters keep their old value until the pedal moves.
- **The GR meter's words**: `tools/features/gr.py` exports `SHOWN` (data + 20) and `LAYOUT` (data + 40: Position in bits 8..15, `gr.POSITIONS`; Mtr Size in px in bits 24..30), which `gr_meter.s` already kept for its paint; the GR code is unchanged. `exp_display.py` puts both addresses in `exp.inc` when the GR meter is built (0 otherwise), and the paint reads `LAYOUT` only while `SHOWN` is set. The frame thread writes them; a paint between the meter's own layout and a change of them draws one frame by the newer place, and the meter's own repaint request puts it right: a top view paint after a change from Middle XL to M, before the next Home paint, draws the big value with its rows 124..127 over the XL band still on the screen, until that Home paint lays the band out at M and draws the value again. The meter's before-handler runs first on the shared Home hook, so the display reads `LAYOUT` no earlier than the meter's layout did: when they differ, the display has the newer word, and the meter lays out (and so redraws, dirty) the same place in the next paint. A font switch wipes the old box, rows 124..155, in the Home paint in which the meter lays out the band at L or XL, before its draw. [hypothesis: not seen on the unit] The meter moving while a view other than the top view (the TogglEXP alert) is in list B leaves black notches in its lights: the Home paint then wipes nothing, so the first paints after that view goes wipe the old bars and boxes over lights the meter has already drawn at its new place and does not draw again while they stay lit. The old bars' wipe (rows 0..116) reaches the lights at Left, Right and Bottom, and the band at Middle at every size (row 116 at S, rows 108..116 at XL); the old boxes' wipe reaches the lights at Left and Right, and at Middle L or XL the band's rows from 124 up. Under the bank view the bars follow the meter, so it leaves none there.
