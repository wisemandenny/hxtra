# More snapshots (`HX_MORE_SNAPSHOTS`, `tools/features/more_snapshots.py`, `src/m4/more_snapshots.s`)

Six snapshots: a second bank, B (4..6), on the same three footswitches, kept in the preset file. Snapshot BankSel picks how the footswitches reach it: Hold (the default, below) or Tap, the lit footswitch pressed again (stage 1). Status tags as in [conventions.md](../conventions.md).

- **More snapshots, stage 1** **[verified on unit]** (first MD5 `e9d4f2f800944df1190d2a3915620cfa`, latest `09c73e45264839bf980162b9d1cf1033`, 2026-11-06): pressing the lit snapshot footswitch again flips between bank A (1-3) and bank B (4-6). The redirected tail calls at `0x28dd1640` and `0x28de59d8` swap the three records with a shadow bank in the cave, puts the record being left at another index j, sets current to j, and calls select. So select sees an ordinary change, diffs from the sound being left, and fills an unused bank B record from it. Afterwards j's record and the previous index are put back. Bank B starts as three default records named `SNAPSHOT 4..6`, made on the first flip after each preset load (the parser's start, chained, resets it). A save in bank B flips to bank A for the serializer, then back (below). Next, prev, MIDI CC 69 and HX Edit act on the live bank. Bank B lives in RAM only.

- **Bank view on a hold (Snapshot BankSel)** **[verified offline]** (2026-11-18, branch `snapshot-bank-view`; the module, and the built footswitch handler, function dispatch, 30-tick timer callback, deferred dispatch and Home layout, run in unicorn by `tests/test_more_snapshots.py`): a virtual setting, ID `0xe6` (container 114, bits 0..1), in Global Settings > Footswitches, label `BankSel\nSnapshot`: 0 Hold (the default, read while unset), 1 Tap (the flip above, unchanged). In Hold, a press of FS1..3 selects nothing; its click (type 3, posted after the release when no hold fired) selects snapshot k in the bank the footswitches point at; a 1 s hold of FS1 points them at bank A, FS2 at bank B, FS3 at nothing. That bank is `snaps_data` byte 4, VIEW: 0 A or 1 B, the bank itself, whichever is live. The footswitches point at the other bank when Snapshot BankSel is Hold and byte 4 is not byte 0 (the live bank); on Tap every hook ignores byte 4. The pieces:
  - **Events**: `0x28dd1986`, the handler `0x28dd1978`'s `bl 0x28df0280` (r4 the event, r5 the handler's object), goes to `snaps_event`, which calls the getter first and returns its result. Every event reaches it, with or without long press: long press owns only the handler's first 4 bytes, and its stock trampoline comes back at `0x28dd197c`. A PRESS arms footswitch k (`snaps_armed`) when its function is 14, setting 21 is 3, Snapshot BankSel is Hold and the tuner is not open (a press there only leaves it, `0x28dd19f8`), and no learn waits: the dispatch first reads `0x28dd87a0(0x28d96084())` (`[0x28128b4c+0x14]`, Bypass Assign's footswitch learn, armed by `0x28dd87ac` at `0x28dbbbe6`) and `0x28dde9ec(0x28d9608c())` (`[0x28125d30+0x24b8]`, the CC learn of Bypass Assign, `0x28ddea4c`, and of Controller Assign, `0x28dde9f8` at `0x28dbccd6`), and while either is set gives the press to the learn and selects nothing (`0x28dd14a8..0x28dd14be`, `0x28dd1500`, `0x28dd1530`). The footswitch learn calls its callback from the dispatch (`0x28dda4b8`), and the callbacks (`0x28dbb8bd`, `0x28dbb8d9`) clear them, so they are read at the press, not at the click. It reads the state stock reads next (`0x28dd1a24..0x28dd1a5a`): with `[obj+0x6d]` clear and another switch pending (`[obj+0x4f]`, `[obj+0x50]`) that is still pressed (`[obj+0x78+p]`), stock makes the FS1+FS2 or FS2+FS3 combo (setting 21 to 2, `0x28dd0f44`) or drops both (FS1+FS3) and dispatches neither, so neither is armed. A pending switch already let go was selected on its own click, and stock drops the new press's dispatch, so the new one is armed. A CLICK or HOLD reads and clears the arm, then checks the same gates again. Touches (codes 9..11) are left to stock.
  - **Held back**: `snaps_select` (the dispatch's tail call `0x28dd1640`) returns 0 for function 14 in Hold, with no lock and no select. Next and previous (cases 7 and 8) share that call, so `snaps_next_cur` and `snaps_prev_cur` now mark every step that does not wrap (`snaps_data` byte 5 = 3, STEP), which `snaps_select` and `snaps_cc_select` clear and send to stock select. The snapshot action's call `0x28de59d8` (the Command Center) has its own handler: stock select in Hold, `snaps_select` on Tap. A known race: if a step in the key task (FS4, FS5) marks STEP and the request task's deferred dispatch of an FS1..3 press reaches `snaps_select` before the step's own select, the press takes the mark and selects at its dispatch and again on its click, and the step is held back [hypothesis: not seen; the window is a few instructions].
  - **Select on the click** (`snaps_click`): in the bank the footswitches point at, `snaps_cross`; in the live bank, stock select, so the lit one does what Reselect Snapshot (ID 103) says, but for going back to a snapshot of the other bank (below); select held off (`+0x9a9`): stock select, which refuses it. Then byte 4 takes byte 0, the bank now live (a refused select leaves the live bank as it was, and the footswitches go back to it). The click holds D, the DSP model's lock, from before it reads byte 2 (SAVING) until after the cross has flipped byte 0, and every serializer holds D from before its flip to bank A until after the flip back (next item): a click during a serializer (a save, or HX Edit's Get edit buffer from the transport task) waits for the flip back and then selects in the bank heard, and a serializer waits for a click [verified offline]. Between a serializer's flips (SAVING: `snaps_saving` sets it before the flip to bank A, `snaps_saved` clears it after the flip back, so it spans both selects) a click selects nothing and leaves byte 4, for when nothing takes D (before the boot makes the DSP model): there byte 0 is bank A while bank B is heard, the flip back would move whatever it selected to bank B, and a cross would swap the records the serializer is reading. Select and `snaps_cross` run in the key task under the listener mutex, as stock's Stomp-mode hold (`0x28dd1f2c` → `0x28de59a8` → `0x28de59d8`) does. A short tap (let go inside the 30-tick window) selects on its click; the deferred dispatch after it finds the switch released and does nothing. Two switches pressed more than 30 ticks apart each select on their click: the last let go wins, where stock selects in press order.
  - **Exclusion with the serializers: D** [verified offline] (2026-11-19, PR #168's review; the stock facts, by disassembly, are in [m4/snapshots.md](../m4/snapshots.md) under Locks; the module run in unicorn by `tests/test_more_snapshots.py`, D as the ROM's recursive mutex with an owner and a depth, a task that would wait stopped and resumed): HX Edit's Get edit buffer runs the serializer in the transport task, under D, and before this nothing in the module took D.
