# GR meter (`HX_GR=1`, `tools/features/gr.py`, `src/m4/gr_meter.s`, `src/sharc/frame_probe.asm`, `tools/patch_comp.py`)

Ten lights on the Home screen and the compressor's Edit view showing a compressor block's gain reduction, set by that block's own knobs. How it works end to end; the image-by-image history is in [gr-meter-log.md](gr-meter-log.md). Status tags as in [conventions.md](../conventions.md).

## What it does

- Ten lights at 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 14, 19 and 25 dB of gain reduction, green to red (`00ff00` .. `ff0000`), on the Home divider (Middle) or along an edge of the screen (Top, Left, Bottom, Right). Middle, Top and Bottom are 30 px lights at a 32 px pitch, Middle in place of the divider's four segments; Left and Right are 21 px lights at a 24 px pitch, clear of the divider, which then stays stock. [verified on unit: Two-pass, Middle-position and Paint-layout images]
- Each compressor block gets three knobs: **Mtr Pos** (Middle, Top, Left, Bottom, Right), **Mtr Dir** (Normal: left to right or top to bottom; Reverse) and **Mtr Size** (Off, S, M, L, XL: 0, 5, 10, 15, 20 px, `gr_meter.s` multiplying the step by 5; Off is the default, so the meter is opt-in). Their preset keys are `GRPosition`, `GRDirection`, `GRThickness`. There are no Global Settings. [verified on unit: Value-at-R8, Mtr-names and Mtr Size images] A preset saved with an old Mtr Thk value above 4 shows XL [hypothesis: the firmware clamps it to the list, else gr_meter.s does]. Stock knob short names run to 8 characters, spaces included ("Hi Shelf", "Vol Trim") [verified offline], and "Mtr Size" is 8; the 9-character "Meter Thk" did not display properly [verified on unit].
- Blocks (`COMP_MODELS` in `tools/patch_comp.py`), mono and stereo: Deluxe Comp and Rochester Comp (core `0x271a`), LA Studio Comp (`0x2719`), Kinky Comp, Ampeg Opto Comp and Red Squeeze (`0x2710`). Each was checked on the unit; see their entries in the log.
- The meter shows the core that was last given a Mtr Size above Off (`GR_CORE`). With several compressors in a preset, only that one is measured. [verified on unit: Metered-core image]
- **Edit view** (issue #78) [verified on unit: gr-meter-edit-2 (MD5 `2e20c82e5833ccf6a56652cf96075b3e`, `HX_GR=1 HX_DSP_METER=1`): every Mtr Pos, Dir and Size, other blocks, the model browser, a split path, stereo models, the DSP meter alongside]: while the selected block is one of `COMP_MODELS` (matched by its block object's model name, `+0x54`, against the Entry 3 string indices `gr.comp_names()` puts in `gr.inc`), the same ten lights show in its Edit view at the Home geometry, Mtr Pos, Dir and Size as set. With two metered compressors, both Edit views show the measured one.

## How it works

1. **Knobs** (`tools/patch_comp.py`, [sharc/model-blobs.md](../sharc/model-blobs.md)): each model gets three integer parameters after its own, in Entry 3, its blob's parameter map and its Entry 0 row, with writers to the core as pids 100..102, and an init record per core, pid 103, saying whether the composite wires the core's output over its first input or its second. A model whose core had no writer gets a path word in its parameter map.
2. **DSP** (`src/sharc/frame_probe.asm`, [sharc/registry.md](../sharc/registry.md)): `tools/block3.py` places the wrapper in Block 3's room beside NAM's slots, under the Metronome and the FX Loop gate (`dsp_place()`; it doesn't fit beside three slots, so `HX_GR=1` builds NAM with two unless `HX_NAM_SLOTS` says fewer) and points each core's registry entry at a wrapper (`ENTRY_271A`, `ENTRY_2719`, `ENTRY_2710`) and its control slot at our own (`CTRL_*`). The control function, called as (pid R4, pointer to the value R8, the core's state R12), keeps pids 100..103 per core, and for `0x2710` the core's Release, in one table of 16 cores (`CORES`: state, Mtr Pos, Dir, Size, Release coefficient, output in `I1`), replaced in turn when full; every other pid goes to the stock function. The process wrapper sends every core but the metered one straight to its stock code; for the metered core it calls the stock code and takes the peak of its input before the call and of its output after it (`I1` or `I2`, by the core's `CORES` entry: both inputs change in the call, so the output can't be found at run time); for the Deluxe Comp, A is 1 / the core's own gain `PM(1, I8)`. It keeps the largest of each over a 64-block window (about 21 ms).
3. **The frame** ([m4/dsp-link.md](../m4/dsp-link.md)): the wrapper writes into every outgoing frame's tail: 200 the marker, 216 and 217 the window peaks, 218 the knobs (tag `0x6B`, then Position, Direction, Thickness; 0 with no metered core), 250 the window count, 251 the Release coefficient to the 1024th power. The Release comes from the Deluxe core's state, the Kinky core's entry, or else a fixed 500 ms. Words 201..215, 219..249 and 252 are not written (the M4 reads none of them; 252, the Attack coefficient, stopped being read when the meter began showing the measured reduction as is). It keeps up to 16 frames, each checked for its last word as it is found. A tag in word 253 never reached the M4 [verified on unit: Where-lost and Word-218 images]. Nothing clears the tail when the metered core stops running (a preset without it): the stock sender rewrites only the head, the messages and the trailer ([m4/dsp-link.md](../m4/dsp-link.md)), so the frames keep the core's last words, marker included [verified offline: disassembly].
4. **M4** (`src/m4/gr_meter.s`, `tools/features/gr.py`, [m4/home-screen.md](../m4/home-screen.md)): four handlers on shared hooks: `frame` before the DSP stream's receive callback, `construct` after the Home constructor (46 widgets of its own, hidden), and `erase` and `draw` around the Home paint. The frame handler turns the peaks into dB with integer maths (log2 in 1/256 octaves, within 0.14 dB; the thread has no FPU context of its own), shows the reduction as measured both ways (it already carries the comp's attack and release), and in silence (input below -72 dB) lets it decay at the comp's Release. It turns the meter off once word 250, the window count, has not changed for 1024 frames: a running core closes a window every 64 blocks whatever the signal, while the marker stays after the core stops (issue #176) [verified on unit, 2026-11-20: the meter lit at Middle while playing, then a select of a preset without a compressor; with this rule (MD5 `9cb6be692b2ba9cb75817a772a83ffbe`) the band was gone and the breathing line back 1 s later, and the same at 5 and 10 s, where `next` 4d8e360 (MD5 `34016dc96f63e443f011c0f9da9caebb`) left it lit at 1, 5 and 10 s; see the Stale tail entry in [gr-meter-log.md](gr-meter-log.md)]. The count of those frames stops at its top, so it never wraps back under 1024 [verified offline: `tests/test_gr_preset_change.py`, the wrapper in `ssim` feeding the M4 code in unicorn through a model of the stock sender]. It only records the layout (lights lit, Position, Direction, Thickness) and asks for a repaint when that changes; the paint applies it to the widgets under the widget lock, as the stock paint does, so no setter runs on the DSP stream's thread. 64 frames after the meter first shows, the paint runs the stock Home layout `0x28dc87cc(home, 0)` once, or the footswitch labels stay blank. While the meter shows, the breathing line yields to it (`SHOWN`).
5. **Edit view** (`edit_top` after the Edit view top half's paint, literal `0x28d96da0`, shared with dsp_meter; `edit_params` after the generic list A paint, literal `0x28d96e38`, only for the Edit view `0x28128270`): both run `edit`, which needs the layout to show, list A painting the Edit view and list B its top half (not the model browser; [m4/home-screen.md](../m4/home-screen.md)), and the selected block (`0x28df0280`) to be a metered model. No widget: it writes the lights straight into the framebuffer after whichever half painted last, since list A's paint can draw over them and the frame's `(0, 1)` repaints only list A. Only lit lights are drawn: an unlit one writes back what it covered and then leaves the screen alone, since a black light over text cut the text [verified on unit: gr-meter-edit-1]. Under each lit light it keeps the pixels it covered (`esave`, 600 a light): a pixel that no longer holds the colour it drew was drawn by a stock paint, so it saves that one before covering it again, and when a light dims, moves or goes it writes back only the pixels still in its colour.

## Standards

- **GR-meter standards** (`docs/gr-meter-standards.md`): meter the applied gain; keep the largest GR per display update; instant rise; do not apply the comp's attack again; 10 lights, non-linear, 1 dB first step.

## Known limits

- The Deluxe Comp in a playing preset reads low (Metered-core and Gain-read images); the Rochester Comp, on the same core, does not [verified on unit].
- The Sweep Echo, the Busy One amps and preamps and the 3-Band Comp (three `0x271a` cores per channel) run the same cores, so they enter the wrapper too. Every core but the metered one jumps straight to its stock code, 15 instructions over the stock core alone, and passes its own pids on (see Shared cores in [sharc/cores/compressors.md](../sharc/cores/compressors.md)).
- **Issue #84** [verified on unit, 2026-11-16]: on v1.1.0 a preset near the DSP limit (stock Table 1 costs summing to about 9026: SV Beast, dual cab, Regal Bass DI, Retro Reel, Dimension, Graphic EQ, a mono and a stereo 3-Band Comp) froze the HX Stomp on load: hxtra-7e boot-1 (v1.1.0 with the setup page's features, MD5 `a755cbaa…`) froze, the same without `HX_GR` (hxtra-7e-2, `7ff6336a…`) did not, and the preset without its two 3-Band Comps did not on the flashed v1.1.0. Then every core ran the wrapper's peak and pack work (about 380 instructions a call on v1.1.0, 160 after gr-slim, in `ssim`) and the frame scan (about 2,000 to 2,500 a call until the scan ends). **[hypothesis]** The nine cores' share pushed a preset already at the limit past the DSP's block time. The fix, hxtra-7e-3 (`next` 268b259 with the fix and v1.1.0's setup-page features, MD5 `86eca997…`) [verified on unit]: the preset loads and reloads without a freeze, and a Deluxe Comp's meter still lights at Threshold −40 dB and goes out at 0 dB.

The compressor cores: [sharc/cores/compressors.md](../sharc/cores/compressors.md). Frame words: [m4/dsp-link.md](../m4/dsp-link.md).
- **Size** [verified offline]: 150 DM words and 419 instructions, 780 normal words of Block 3 (789 before other cores went straight to the stock code, 1,236 before the gr-slim cleanup, which dropped the debug frame words, the 96-candidate list and the state copies, and merged the three per-core tables). `tests/test_gr.py` `test_dsp_stays_small` holds it under 800.
